## Direction contract

THESIS: Firmo is the title screen of a 1986 arcade game, because the wait there is a game: fliperama, cold beer, PS4. Booking is PRESS START, services are a select screen, reviews are the high-score table. Refuses the dark-leather-and-brass barbershop with a split hero and a row of service cards.

OWN-WORLD: indigo-to-void night sky, neon magenta horizon, banded orange-yellow sun sitting on a cyan wireframe grid running forward, black mountain silhouettes at the edges. Display in extruded italic caps with magenta-to-purple faces and stepped depth; labels in wide-tracked cyan caps; body in a wide readable sans on void. Controls are grid rows and chamfered neon frames; the active one lights magenta along its full width. Scanlines over photos.

STORY: Bruno sees his shop's name as a game title, then his own waiting room photographed with HUD pins on the PS4, the Mario fridge, the coffee and the sign wall, then his 4,9 as a high score, and a booking console that writes the WhatsApp message. He believes this sells his difference; he replies.

FIRST VIEWPORT: phone portrait. Demo strip, then HUD bar (sun mark + FIRMO left, START button right). Sky fills; FIRMO extruded across ~90% width at upper third, BARBEARIA · CENTRO DE PRUDENTE tracked cyan under it; banded sun on the horizon at ~58%; grid below scrolling toward the viewer. On the grid: the hook line (Hora marcada, ar gelado, cerveja na geladeira e fliperama na espera), a score line 4,9 ★ · 126 avaliações · Ter–Sáb, and the primary magenta chamfered button "Marcar meu horário" with a blinking PRESS START cue. Desktop: same axis, centered, larger.

FORM: Retro synth cover on a receding grid (dealt challenger, bolder register re-roll 1, user choice; seed key ad16a6f0). Signature interaction: the grid's forward speed follows scroll velocity and the sun sets as the page scrolls; the select-screen booking console composes the WhatsApp message.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
