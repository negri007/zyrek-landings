## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the store's own photo wall: the blue-and-white checkered "Planet Patas" backdrop every pet poses in front of after the bath, on the blue grooming table. It refuses the pastel pet-shop template (cream cards, cartoon icon tiles, stock dogs).

OWN-WORLD: Royal blue #0A4C9E fields with lighter paw-print wallpaper (the logo's own ground); checker tiles alternating blue and white; Baloo 2 ExtraBold white headlines over a white band carrying the red #D2232E subtitle (their Instagram post format); red pill buttons; orange #F07A3C secondary; photos as polaroids with a paw stamp; tiny lilac and yellow paws only as decoration.
